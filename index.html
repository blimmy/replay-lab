BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few little
things to know.

Can I order a real product?

This is an interactive concept. Prices and reservations demonstrate the proposed experience; no money is taken and no product is ordered.

How would a collection be made?

The proposed business would confirm supplier samples, costs and delivery terms, then open pre-orders. A batch would proceed only when its announced threshold is met; otherwise buyers would receive refunds under the stated terms.

Where do my designs and answers go?

Saved designs stay in your browser. Shared design links exclude your private memory text. Survey answers must be downloaded and voluntarily shared with the researcher; they are not submitted to a server.
